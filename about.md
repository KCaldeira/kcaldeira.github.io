This is the personal blog of Ken Caldeira. Things I post here are my own
thoughts, often half-formed, and are not statements on behalf of any
organization I am affiliated with.

I coordinate the [Conceptual Investigations Unit](https://ciunit.org), and I am
a [Visiting Scholar at Stanford University](https://sustainablesolutions.stanford.edu/people/ken-caldeira/),
housed in Steve Davis's
[Sustainable Solutions Lab](https://sustainablesolutions.stanford.edu). I am
also a Senior Scientist at Gates Ventures.

Previously, I was a Senior Scientist in the Department of Global Ecology at the
[Carnegie Institution for Science](https://bse.carnegiescience.edu/dr-ken-caldeira-emeritus),
and before that I worked in the Energy and Environment Directorate of Lawrence
Livermore National Laboratory. I did a postdoc in the Department of Geosciences
at Penn State University. I hold a PhD and Master's in atmospheric sciences
from New York University, and a BA from Rutgers University, where I majored in
philosophy.

My professional biography and a list of what I have published are on the
[CIunit site](https://ciunit.org/ken-caldeira.html).

Elsewhere: [Bluesky](https://bsky.app/profile/kencaldeira.com) and
[LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/in/ken-caldeira-2a45648/).
